---
description: Break down my complex request into clear steps
---

Before acting, decompose my request into clear, actionable steps:

1. **Extract the goal**: What am I actually trying to achieve?
2. **Identify sub-tasks**: Break this into logical steps
3. **Dependencies**: What needs to happen first?
4. **Decision points**: Where do I need to make choices?
5. **Risks/gotchas**: What could go wrong?

Output format:
```
## Goal
[One sentence: what we're achieving]

## Steps (in order)
1. [Step 1 - what, why, dependencies]
2. [Step 2 - what, why, dependencies]
...

## Decision Points
- [Question 1] - Options: A, B, C
- [Question 2] - Options: X, Y, Z

## Risks to Consider
- [Risk 1 and mitigation]
- [Risk 2 and mitigation]

## Proceed?
Respond with:
- "Yes, proceed as planned" - I'll execute all steps
- "Modify step X to..." - I'll adjust and confirm
- "Let's discuss..." - We'll refine the plan
```

Wait for my approval before proceeding.
